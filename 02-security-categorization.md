# Security Categorization

The system was categorized using FIPS 199 impact levels.

## Confidentiality: Moderate

The system processes PII and financial information. Unauthorized disclosure could harm citizens and damage public trust.

## Integrity: High

Incorrect or manipulated data could result in improper benefit distribution, financial loss, or legal consequences.

## Availability: Moderate

System downtime would delay services but would not result in immediate life-threatening impact.

---

Overall Security Categorization:
Moderate (with High integrity impact)

This categorization drives control selection in the next phase.
