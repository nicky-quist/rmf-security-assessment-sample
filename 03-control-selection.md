# Control Selection

Controls were selected based on NIST SP 800-53 for a Moderate baseline system, with emphasis on integrity protection.

## Key Controls Selected

### AC-2 – Account Management
Ensures proper account provisioning, review, and removal.

### IA-2 – Identification and Authentication
Multi-factor authentication required for privileged users.

### AU-6 – Audit Review and Analysis
Security logs reviewed regularly to detect suspicious activity.

### SI-4 – System Monitoring
Continuous monitoring of network and host-level activity.

### CM-6 – Configuration Settings
Standardized secure configurations enforced across systems.

### RA-5 – Vulnerability Monitoring
Routine vulnerability scanning and remediation tracking.

---

Control tailoring was applied to focus more heavily on identity protection, logging, and monitoring due to the system’s integrity requirements.
